## 🏭 About Elastofirm

Elastofirm is a European group specialised in the compounding and extrusion of rubber and silicone, and in liquid silicone rubber (LSR) technology. From our head office in Hilversum and 10 production sites in the Netherlands, Germany and Spain, our team of 500+ rubber specialists develops high-performance compounds, precision profiles, tubes and seals for customers across Europe.

We work for customers in building & construction, transport & aerospace (automotive, railway and aerospace) and general industry, as well as household appliances, lighting and pharma. Our services cover the full chain: engineering, material development, compounding, tooling, extrusion and finishing.

Over the past decade, Elastofirm has grown by bringing specialised rubber and silicone companies together under one roof. Each company keeps its own expertise and personal touch, backed by the strength, scale and consistent European quality standards of the group. Together we bring more than 250 years of combined experience.

## 💻 Why we're on GitHub

A tailor-made profile starts with the right compound, the right data and the right process. As a growing group of companies, we invest in digital tools that connect our sites, share knowledge across the group and make working with us faster and more transparent for our customers.

This organization is where our engineering and IT teams collaborate on:

- 🧪 **Compound & recipe data** — material development, formulation and lab data management
- 🏭 **Production & quality tooling** — MES, traceability and quality systems across our plants
- 🔌 **APIs & integrations** — connecting group companies, ERPs, suppliers and customers
- 📊 **Data & reporting** — group-wide insight into operations, sales and performance
- 🧰 **Internal tools & automation** — the practical tooling that keeps a multi-site manufacturer running

Public repositories are open for issues and pull requests. Internal projects live in private repositories.

## ✅ Quality & responsibility

Customers trust us with critical sealing and insulation applications, so quality is non-negotiable. Across the group, our sites hold certifications including ISO 9001 (quality), ISO 14001 (environment), ISO 45001 (health & safety), ISO 50001 (energy) and IATF 16949 (automotive). We work with our customers on more than the product alone — including sustainability, waste reduction and working capital.
